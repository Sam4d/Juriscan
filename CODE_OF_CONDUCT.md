# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and precise. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks and publishing private information are not accepted. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Respect maintainers' and contributors' time and different backgrounds.

Use public issues for non-sensitive conduct concerns or requests for a private contact channel. Do not disclose another person's private information. The owner may moderate content or participation to protect contributors and should explain decisions when doing so is safe. This project does not yet provide a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting service; one should be established as the community grows.
